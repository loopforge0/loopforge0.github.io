About Loop Forge

Loop Forge is a hobby project testing open-weight AI models in ComfyUI, by someone with no background in film making. Video so far, and every project gets a page here with the prompts, workflows and settings it used.

The house rules: every clip plays uncut, every prompt is published exactly as it was sent to the model, and the parts that did not work stay in.

The prompt

A deliberately texture-dense close-up, for the resolution ladder.

The point of this prompt is to put as much fine structure in front of the model as one frame can
hold — skin pores and freckles, fine flyaway hair in two dye colours, tattoo line work, engraved
metal, knitted wool — so that a resolution that cannot resolve detail has somewhere obvious to
fail. The existing R1 portrait is a good photograph but a soft one, and §36 showed the difference
between 1 MP and 4.3 MP on it is real but needs looking for.

Written to the PE-T2I spec: one observational paragraph, present tense, describing what is in the
frame rather than instructing a camera.

No text anywhere in it, on purpose. §35: a prompt that asks for text it cannot spell out turns the
output into an unattributable result, and a tattoo is exactly where lettering would creep in.

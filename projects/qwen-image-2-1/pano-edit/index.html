The prompt

Turn this into a 360 degree panorama in equirectangular projection. Keep the woman exactly as she is, at the centre of the frame with her arm reaching down toward the camera, and build the whole surrounding place around her: the paved square continuing behind the camera so it fills the frame from edge to edge, the trees and buildings running all the way around the horizon, the paving stretching and curving outward toward the bottom edge, and the sky stretching and curving outward toward the top edge. The horizon is a straight horizontal line across the middle of the frame, and the left edge continues seamlessly into the right edge.

Notes

2880×1440 is only about 600×600 pixels inside a 75 degree view, because a 360 image is mostly behind you. A wrap-padded 2x upscale gets to 5760×2880 without breaking the seam.

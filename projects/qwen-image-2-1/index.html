Qwen-Image 2.1, run locally

Qwen-Image 2.1 generates and edits with one 7 billion parameter model, and its autoencoder carries an alpha channel, so transparency comes out of the model rather than a background remover. Everything below ran locally on an RTX 3060, with every prompt exact and every timing measured.

Verdict

The outfit made it, but the face did not. It drifts toward a drawing. At strength 0 it ignores the reference completely, and at 4 it gets closer but it is still not her. For a real person I'd skip it.

Notes

The official MOSS edit template switched to Base with CharRef v2, at the card's settings: edit_format ai-toolkit, 50 steps, CFG 3, euler / sgm_uniform, empty negative. It loads natively, without the Ostris node. Image strength (on Text Encode MOSS-Image Edit) sets how hard it looks at the reference; the three images differ only in that setting.

The reference goes straight into the text encoder, in front of the prompt. Its card says it is made for anime.

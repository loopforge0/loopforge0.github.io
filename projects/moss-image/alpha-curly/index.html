Notes

paralaif's own workflow from the LoRA's repository: MOSS SFT with the 8-step DMD LoRA, CFG 1, and a second decode through the RGBA VAE. The text encoder is swapped to the w4a8 file. genai-craft's LoRA goes in the same graph with its card's lcm sampler. The workflow saves an RGB and an RGBA image; the RGBA one is shown here on a checkerboard and is the download.

Without a LoRA the checkerboard is painted into the picture, and the cut-out comes out partly see-through. genai-craft's card says it was only tested with its own script, so it may do better there than in ComfyUI.

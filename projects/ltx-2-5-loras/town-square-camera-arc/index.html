Limit

I built the layout in Blender from primitives and made the look still from its first frame with Qwen-Image 2.1. The camera arc, the fountain, the tree and the lamp land where the layout put them. The box mannequin has no front, so she turns her back mid-shot. The nearest bench smears, and the empty sky in the layout became more houses.

A young woman with long curly red hair, in a cream knitted sweater and blue jeans, stands beside a weathered sandstone fountain in a small European town square at golden hour as the camera slowly arcs around her and moves closer. Cream and ochre townhouses with shuttered windows, a café awning, a leafy plane tree, black iron street lamps, wooden benches and worn cobblestones, warm low sunlight with long soft shadows. Match the first frame. Photoreal.

Notes

I have seen more side effects like these on busier layout animations, so I expect it to work better with simpler camera moves. The prompt matches the look still and leaves out the words clay and 3D.

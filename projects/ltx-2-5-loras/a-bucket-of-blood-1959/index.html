Works

This is a real black-and-white film, so there is no colour original to check against. Every colour I named in the prompt lands, from the dusty-rose cardigan to the yellow lampshade, and the faces hold through the shot. The cane comes out red without being asked for.

The prompt

Reference shows a cluttered 1950s bohemian apartment where a smiling young woman with wavy shoulder-length hair, in a fitted cardigan, a wide belt and a dark skirt, holds a wicker handbag beside a grinning young man in a dark sweater and jeans, while a man in a dark suit with a cane stands by the doorway behind them and a tall sculpture draped in a sheet fills the right of the frame, rendered in monochrome film. Edited shows the same scene with natural colors restored. COLORIZE the woman with strawberry-blonde hair in a dusty-rose cardigan, a black leather belt and a charcoal skirt with a pale straw handbag, the young man with light-brown hair in a navy sweater and faded blue jeans, the man by the door in a black suit, an off-white canvas sheet over the sculpture, pale sage-green walls and cream doors, a warm yellow lampshade glowing above a dark wooden dresser, a worn red patterned sofa and warm natural skin tones. Subject identity, framing, and background geometry are identical to the reference; only color information differs between reference and edited.

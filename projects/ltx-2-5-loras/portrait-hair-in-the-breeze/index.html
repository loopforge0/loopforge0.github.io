CINEMAGRAPH_MOTION, tripod locked-off static camera, zero camera movement, the woman is frozen, her face, eyes, smile, head, knitted sweater, the bookshelves and the window remain completely frozen, only a few loose strands of her wavy brown hair sway gently in a soft breeze, everything else stays perfectly still, seamless natural loop.

camera movement, pan, tilt, zoom, parallax, whole image moving, background sliding, person moving, face moving, flicker on entire image, noisy texture, crawling texture, distorted, blurry, low quality

Notes

Writing the prompt in the card's order, with what is frozen first and then what moves, made the face slightly stiller than my first prompt did. The trigger is CINEMAGRAPH_MOTION, at strength 1.5.

Run it yourself

Download everything for this test (.zip, 7.2 MB). It holds the workflow, every input it loads and a README. Copy the inputs into ComfyUI/input, put the models below in place and drag the workflow onto ComfyUI.

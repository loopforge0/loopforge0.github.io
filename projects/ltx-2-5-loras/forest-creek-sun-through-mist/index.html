Works

Both versions are pulled down 2.5 stops so that an SDR screen can show the highlights. The SDR source has nothing above white, so the sunlit water turns flat grey. The HDR version keeps the glints and the light shafts. That headroom is the model's estimate of what was there, not recovered data.

Works

This uses the card's prompt template on a clip of a dry sandbox. Water pools around their knees and the bucket pours water instead of sand. The sand around the edges stays dry, and the cream trousers on the right come out as light jeans.

The prompt

Download workflowDownload everything for this test (.zip, 10.5 MB) 
Reference shows two young women kneeling side by side in a wooden sandbox full of dry golden sand in a sunny backyard, scooping sand in their hands, pouring sand from a red bucket and patting the sand. Edited shows the same scene with water added. ADD WATER clear shallow water flooding the whole sandbox and completely covering the sand up to their knees, rippling around their legs; water streaming out of their cupped hands and dripping from their fingers, the red bucket pouring a stream of water that splashes into the flooded sandbox, and their palms slapping the water surface into splashes and spreading rings of ripples. Subject identity, clothing, framing, and background geometry are identical to the reference; only water-related elements differ between reference and edited.

Notes

I also tried to replace all of the sand with water and could not get that to work. This LoRA is strict about the prompt format it expects. The trigger is ADD WATER.

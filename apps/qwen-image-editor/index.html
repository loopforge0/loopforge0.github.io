Qwen Image Editor

Edit and create images with Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo on your own computer, in a simple editor or from Claude Desktop, ChatGPT and Goose.

Download for WindowsDownload for macOS
  • Windows NVIDIA graphics card with 12 GB+, driver 580 or newer, 32 GB RAM recommended, about 25 GB free disk
  • macOS Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or newer
Source on GitHubAll releases

The installers don't include the model. On first start the editor checks your computer, you accept Qwen's licence on Hugging Face, and the model downloads to your machine.

What it can do

Point at what to change

Circle parts of an image in colours and describe each change. The circles are removed from the result.

Replace the earring in the red circle with a small silver stud, and remove the tattoo in the green circle. Keep everything else unchanged.

See what changed

Compare with a slider, side by side, or a Changes view that marks every edited pixel in red.

Transparent images

Make cut-outs and stickers with real transparency, or extract an object from a busy photo.

This is an RGBA image with transparency. Extract the latte cup and saucer from <image1> onto a transparent background. The image has alpha channel and the background is transparent.

Inside your chat app

Claude Desktop, ChatGPT and Goose can generate and edit, and open the editor in the chat so you can step in.

Loop mode

The agent makes an image, reviews it against a checklist, and fixes what fails, round after round until it passes.
